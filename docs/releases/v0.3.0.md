# Space Studio 0.3.0

- **Local (free) engine.** In the model picker, switch to **Local (free)** to use an AI model running on your own computer through Ollama or llama.cpp. It's free, works offline, and nothing is sent to Keplar or counted against your credits. Answers are only as good as the model you run; cloud models (Orbit, Nebula, Solar, Nova) stay one click away.
- **Specialist tracks.** Every request is now routed to one of six specialists — Roblox Luau systems, Blender modeling and materials, lighting and VFX, UI and TweenService, retention and game-loop design, or audio and level geometry — which adds focused instructions and checked reference notes. Each answer shows which specialist handled it.
- **Troubleshooting cards.** When something on your computer fails (the local engine isn't running, a model is missing, a scan times out), Space Studio shows the real error with numbered steps to fix it instead of hanging.

Installers are still not code-signed. Windows SmartScreen and macOS Gatekeeper will warn; on macOS, right-click the app and choose Open. Use SHA256SUMS to check your download.
