# Space Studio 0.3.1

- Fixed: pressing Next or Skip tour in the first-run tour could freeze the window. The tour now moves without per-frame blur effects, and every step change finishes in under a second, even on slower graphics cards.

Installers are still not code-signed. Windows SmartScreen and macOS Gatekeeper will warn; on macOS, right-click the app and choose Open. Use SHA256SUMS to check your download.
