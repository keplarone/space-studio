# Space Studio 0.2.0

A new Space Studio, rebuilt from the sign-in screen up.

- **Sign in with Roblox, Keplar or Google.** Roblox sign-in works for accounts that already have Roblox linked; otherwise sign in with Keplar or Google and link Roblox in Account.
- **Onboarding.** A short setup with your date of birth (required, never shown to anyone) and a few optional questions. Creators under 13 need a parent or guardian to approve before using AI features.
- **A living universe.** The star field from space.keplar.one now fills the app, with real motion blur. Every prompt jumps to warp and shifts the purple aura. Finishing setup flies you into the app, followed by a quick tour.
- **Floating top bar.** Your picture and name on the left (hover for your plan and credits); Robux your experiences earn on the right (hover to watch pending Robux count up).
- **Chat with model and effort.** Pick Orbit, Nebula, Solar or Nova and Low, Medium, High, Extra or Ultracode right in the composer. Ultracode adds a second self-review pass.
- **Creator dashboard.** Add your experiences and see live players (blue) and revenue (green) over 1 hour to 90 days. Player history is recorded by Space Studio from the moment you add a game. Revenue comes from a small script you add to your game, which reports Developer Product and Game Pass sales as they happen.
- **Credits.** One credit is one cent of model work actually done. Free includes 30 credits every 5 hours and 120 a week. Plans and prices are on the Plans page; checkout opens soon.
- No outlines anywhere, 3D main buttons, and smoother transitions throughout.

Installers are still not code-signed. Windows SmartScreen and macOS Gatekeeper will warn; on macOS, right-click the app and choose Open. Use SHA256SUMS to check your download.
